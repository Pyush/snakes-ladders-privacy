Privacy Policy: Snakes & Ladders: Editions

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Snakes & Ladders: Editions ("the app") is a board game for families. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it sends nothing about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

So the game can pick up where you left off, it saves a few things only on your device:

  • your settings (sound, vibration, house rules, accessibility options, the chosen edition);
  • the player names and colours you typed on the setup screen;
  • which edition packs you have bought, so they stay unlocked without an internet connection.

These never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes them.

Purchases

Edition packs are sold through Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad). The store handles the payment: the app never sees your card or payment details. It only receives the store's confirmation that a pack was bought, so it can unlock it. "Restore purchases" asks the store again which packs you own. The stores' own privacy policies cover the payment itself:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and it shows no ads.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. On Android it uses Google Play's billing service for purchases, and the phone's vibration for game feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.
