Privacy Policy: Snakes & Ladders: Editions

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Snakes & Ladders: Editions ("the app") is a board game. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we collect nothing ourselves. The app shows ads from Google, and Google's ad software collects some data to do that. Buying "Remove ads" turns the ads off.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of its own. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it sends nothing about you to us.

Ads

The app shows ads in three places, never while you're playing:

  • a banner at the bottom of the main menu;
  • now and then, a full-screen ad between games (at most every second game, and at most every few minutes);
  • a video you can choose to watch to play a free demo game of an edition you haven't bought.

Buying "Remove ads" turns off the banner and the ads between games, and demo games then start without the video.

These ads come from Google AdMob. When the app starts, it asks Google whether the law where you live needs your consent for ads (for example in Europe), and shows Google's consent form if so. When the app loads or shows an ad, Google's ad software on your phone may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android) or, if you allow it, the identifier for advertisers (on iPhone and iPad);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with the ad (whether you watched it, tapped it or closed it).

Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. How Google uses this information is explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads).
  • On iPhone and iPad you can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Buying Remove ads stops the app from showing ads at all.

What stays on your device

So the game can pick up where you left off, it saves a few things only on your device:

  • your settings (sound, vibration, house rules, accessibility options, the chosen edition);
  • the player names and colours you typed on the setup screen;
  • which edition packs you have bought, and whether you bought "Remove ads", so they work without an internet connection.

These never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes them.

Purchases

Edition packs and "Remove ads" are sold through Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad). The store handles the payment: the app never sees your card or payment details. It only receives the store's confirmation that something was bought, so it can unlock it. "Restore purchases" asks the store again what you own. The stores' own privacy policies cover the payment itself:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The app is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app's ads, contact us and we'll help.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. On Android it uses Google Play's billing service for purchases, the advertising ID permission for Google's ads, and the phone's vibration for game feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.
